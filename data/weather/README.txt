Place EnergyPlus EPW weather files in this directory.

Recommended free sources:
- EnergyPlus Weather Data: https://energyplus.net/weather
- Climate.OneBuilding.Org: http://climate.onebuilding.org/

Example files:
- USA_IL_Chicago-OHare.Intl.AP.725300_TMY3.epw (cold climate)
- USA_CA_Los.Angeles.Intl.AP.722950_TMY3.epw (hot dry)
- GBR_London.Gatwick.037760_IWEC.epw (temperate)
